# Editing the LabGenie website

A plain-language guide for editing the website text. **No coding needed.** If you
can fill in a form, you can edit the site.

---

## 1. Open the editor

Go to:

- **Live site:** `https://<your-site>/keystatic`
- **On a developer's laptop:** `http://localhost:3000/keystatic`

On the live site you'll sign in with **GitHub** the first time. (Ask whoever set
up the site to add you as a collaborator if it won't let you in.)

You'll see a panel called **LabGenie CMS** with a list of sections down the left.

## 2. Pick a section

The sections are numbered in the order they appear on the homepage, top to bottom:

| Section | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **1 · Hero** | The big headline at the very top, the sentence under it, and the two buttons |
| **2 · Problem** | The "Your ERP stores the data…" heading and the three stat cards |
| **3 · Product Vision** | "One platform. Every workflow." heading + sub + button |
| **4 · Why LabGenie** | The "Built from the inside" story and the bullet points |
| **5 · Proof** | The "Trusted by the manufacturers…" heading and intro line |
| **6 · FAQ** | The list of questions and answers near the bottom |
| **7 · Closing CTA** | The final "Getting started isn't" heading and buttons |

Click a section and you'll get a simple form. Each box has a label and a short
note telling you what it's for.

## 3. Make your edit

- Just type in the boxes. What you type is what shows on the site.
- Some boxes have a **length limit** so a headline can't get so long it breaks the
  layout. If a box turns red, you've gone over — shorten it and the red goes away.
- Boxes marked **required** can't be left empty.
- In lists (like the FAQ or the Problem cards) you can **add**, **remove**, and
  **drag to reorder** items.

## 4. Save = publish

Click **Save** (top right).

- On the **live site**, saving publishes your change. It takes about **1–2 minutes**
  for the site to rebuild and show it. **Save once and wait** — refreshing or
  saving again won't make it faster.
- Made a typo? Just edit it again and save again. There's no way to "break" it —
  the worst case is a wrong word, which you fix the same way.

## 5. What you can and can't change here

**You can change:** all the words on the homepage and the FAQ — headings,
paragraphs, button labels, stats, and questions/answers.

**You can't change here (on purpose):** layout, colors, fonts, images, the
animations, and the interactive demo. These are kept out of the editor so the site
can't be accidentally broken. If you need one of those changed — or a new section,
a photo swapped, or anything that isn't a text box here — **that's the signal to
ask a developer.** It's not a limitation you did wrong; those changes just live in
the code.

## 6. Tips

- **Preview before you trust it:** after saving, open the live homepage in another
  tab and scroll to your section.
- **Keep headlines short.** The length limits are a guide — punchy reads better.
- **One change at a time** when you're learning, so it's easy to see what moved.

## Questions?

If something looks wrong after an edit, or you need a change that isn't a text box
in the editor, contact your developer. Nothing you type here can take the site
down — at worst a wrong word, fixed by editing again.
