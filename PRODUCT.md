# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers and operators at F&B ingredient manufacturers — flavors, spices,
oleoresins. Three audiences converge on the site:

- **Quality / QA & compliance leads** who live in COAs, spec sheets, and
  reconciliation against raw-material, internal, and customer standards. Skeptical,
  detail-driven, burned by software that promised automation and delivered overhead.
- **Sales / commercial teams** who triage inbound RFPs and need to answer "do we
  already make something that matches this spec?" fast.
- **Operations / procurement leadership** evaluating whether one chat-native system
  can orchestrate quality, sales, and procurement.

Context of use: evaluating a serious operational tool during the workday, often
after being pointed here by a peer or a sales touch. They arrive cautious and
technical, not browsing. The job to be done is to decide, quickly and with
confidence, whether LabGenie is real enough to warrant a demo.

## Product Purpose

LabGenie is the AI operating system for F&B ingredient manufacturers. It reads
messy COAs and spec sheets, reconciles them across raw-material, internal, and
customer standards, and orchestrates quality, sales, and procurement from a single
chat interface. The reconciliation that once took 3–5 days per batch happens in
minutes.

This marketing site is the front door to that product. Success is a qualified
demo request: a manufacturer who, by the time they reach the contact form, already
trusts that the platform is technically real and built by people who understand
their domain. Credibility is the means; the booked demo is the outcome.

LabGenie is the product of Datadivr, a company transitioning from services to a
product-led business. The site carries that shift: this is Datadivr's flagship
product, and its identity should read as a confident product brand, not a services
firm's side project.

## Brand Personality

Bold, technical, fast — aligned to the Datadivr brand.

- **Voice:** the expert in the room, not the salesperson. Speaks the language of
  QA, compliance, and procurement fluently and precisely. States what the product
  literally does, then proves it. Confident enough to show the work.
- **Tone:** direct and engineered. Energy comes from concrete speed and capability
  (days → minutes, three specs reconciled at once), not from adjectives.
- **Emotional goal:** a skeptical technical buyer should feel "these people
  actually understand my problem, and this is real" — relief and recognition, not
  hype-fatigue.

## Anti-references

- **Generic dark SaaS.** No interchangeable purple-gradient hero, hero-metric
  template, or endless identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- **Legacy enterprise ERP.** Not the dated, cluttered, gray Oracle/SAP industrial
  look this product is meant to replace.
- **Consumer food brand.** Not warm, playful, appetite-appeal CPG marketing. This
  is B2B manufacturing infrastructure.
- **Overhyped AI startup.** No breathless "revolutionary AI," no vague magic-box
  claims, no buzzword maximalism. Show the mechanism, not the magic.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product working, don't describe it.** The reconciliation panel, the
   orchestration chat, the count-up stats — visitors should watch LabGenie do the
   job, not read promises about it. Live demonstration is the strongest credibility
   signal for this audience.
2. **Expert confidence earns the technical buyer.** Use the real vocabulary of the
   domain (moisture, total ash, curcumin, COA, spec reconciliation) precisely.
   Specificity is trust; vagueness reads as not understanding the problem.
3. **Speed is the proof, days → minutes is the spine.** The central transformation
   anchors the narrative. Every section should ladder back to a concrete capability
   and its measurable payoff.
4. **One brand: LabGenie is Datadivr, productized.** Visual and verbal identity
   align to the Datadivr brand guide as the company becomes product-led. Consistency
   with that identity beats standalone novelty.
5. **Credibility is the path to the demo.** Build trust section by section so the
   demo CTA feels like the obvious next step, never a hard sell interrupting the
   pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AAA**.

- Contrast: enhanced ratios (7:1 for body text, 4.5:1 for large text) against the
  dark surfaces. The luminous accent palette must be validated at AAA, especially
  muted/dim text tiers and placeholder text — light-on-dark needs extra care.
- Motion: the site is animation-rich (hero reconciliation, orchestration story,
  marquee, count-up stats). Every animation must be fully gated by
  `prefers-reduced-motion` with a non-motion fallback. No content visibility may
  depend on a motion-triggered reveal firing.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and semantic structure throughout.
  Color must never be the sole carrier of meaning (e.g. spec match / fail states
  need a non-color indicator).
